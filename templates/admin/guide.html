Staff guide

How to use Benrey Library

Everything happens in this admin. This guide covers the tasks your role can do; {% if is_admin_role %}as an administrator you can do all of them. {% else %}as a librarian you run the front desk: lending, returns, extensions and members. The catalogue is read-only for you.{% endif %}

exploreGetting around

  • Sidebar: Dashboard, Loans, Members, Books and Authors. The red number next to Loans is how many are overdue right now.
  • Search sits at the top of every list: search loans by book title, member name or email; books by title, ISBN or author.
  • Filters are behind the Filters button on each list. Combine several, then press Apply.
  • Bulk actions: tick rows in a list, pick an action from the bar that appears, run it. You can select across pages with "Select all".

add_circleLend a book

  1. Open the member (Members, search by name or email).
  2. In Lend a book, start typing the title and pick the book. The due date is filled in ({{ loan_days }} days); change it if needed.
  3. Press Save. You can lend several books in one go with "Add another".

Or use Lend a book on the dashboard and pick both the book and the member. If something is not allowed (no free copy, member suspended, already {{ max_loans }} books out), the form says exactly why and nothing is saved.

assignment_returnTake books back

  1. On the member's page, click "Open this member's active loans", or go to Loans → On loan.
  2. Tick the loans being returned.
  3. Choose Mark selected loans as returned and run it.

The message at the top says how many were returned and lists any that were skipped (for example, already returned). A returned loan is final and cannot be reopened.

more_timeExtend a loan

Tick the loan in Loans and run Extend due date by {{ extension_days }} days. A loan can be extended once, and not after it is overdue; those are skipped and listed in the message.

person_addRegister or find a member

Add member: full name and email are enough. A card number (like LIB-40217735) is assigned on save; write it on the member's card. Emails are unique, ignoring capitals, so the form warns you if the person is already registered. At the desk, search Members or Loans by card number, name or email. The member page shows the card number, fees due and all loans, open ones first.

scheduleChase overdue books

  • Overdue loans lists every book past its due date. Sort by due date to see the oldest first.
  • Members with overdue loans is the list to call.
  • Due in the next 3 days is good for friendly reminders.
  • The late fee is €{{ late_fee }} per day, shown on each loan. Nobody has to mark a loan as overdue: it happens automatically.
  • The Fees due column in Members adds up what each member owes today on books they still have; click the Fees due header (twice) to put the highest bill first, or just ask the assistant "Who has the highest bill?".

library_addManage the catalogue Admin

  1. Add the author first if they are new (Add author).
  2. Add book: title, author(s), the 13-digit ISBN (hyphens are fine), genre and number of copies.
  3. Optionally upload a cover: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB.

To take a book out of circulation, tick it in Books and run Withdraw selected books. Deleting is blocked for books with loan history so history is never lost.

{% endif %} {% if can_change_status %}

blockSuspend a member Admin

Tick members in Members and run Suspend selected members. Suspended members keep their current books but cannot borrow new ones. Reactivate selected members undoes it.

auto_awesomeAsk AI

Press Ask AI in the bottom-right corner of any page. You can ask two kinds of question:

  • About a form: "What does due date mean?", "How many books can a member borrow?". Click into a field first and the assistant knows which one you mean. Answers come from this system's own field descriptions and rules, and show which fields they are based on.
  • About the library right now: "Who has the highest bill?", "Is Dune on the shelf?", "Find member Anna", "How many books are overdue?". The answer comes straight from the database with links to the member, loan or book, and only shows what your role may see.

The AI only decides which question you asked; it never sees member names, emails or fees. If the AI service is down, simple questions still work and field help is shown instead.
